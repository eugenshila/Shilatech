  • Part 2 is for staff (warehouse, sales counter, garage, delivery, finance, HR, managers and administrators).
  • Part 3 is a quick reference and troubleshooting guide.

How this manual was written. It was prepared from the application's source code (pages, role rules and workflow documents in docs/). Screen names and button labels match the code. If your deployment has been customised, small differences are possible.

1.6 VIN checker

VIN checker.

Use your VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) for the best part matching. It is 17 characters and is found on the dashboard (visible through the windscreen), on the door frame, or in the logbook.

  1. Open VIN (from the home page search or the footer link VIN checker).
  2. Type or paste the 17 characters. Rules: numbers and capital letters only; the letters I, O and Q are never used.
  3. Press the search button. The site identifies make, model, year and engine.
  4. You are taken to the Shop with a banner "VIN-assisted search active".
  5. In the Parts catalogue for this vehicle box you can:
  6. Press Browse full catalogue and click a category button, or
  7. Type a description such as brake pads and search.
  8. Catalogue results are references only — stock and price are not confirmed. Press Request this part to send us the part number (and your VIN) through the Contact page.

If the VIN is not recognised, use Search manually to browse by make, model and category.

Part 2 — Staff portal

Staff tools are private. They never appear in search engines and cannot be opened without a staff account.

2.1 Signing in for the first time

Staff sign-in page.

Change-password page shown for a temporary password.

  1. On the website menu press Staff portal (or go to /staff-login).
  2. Enter your Staff email and Password, then press Sign in to staff portal.
  3. You are sent straight to the tools for your role (for example a cashier opens the Sales counter).

Temporary password. Accounts created by the administrator (and accounts whose password was reset) start with a temporary password. On first sign-in you will see "Change your temporary password":

  1. Press Change password (page /staff-password).
  2. Enter the current / temporary password, then the new password twice. The new password must be at least 12 characters.
  3. Press Save my new password. This signs out any other sessions on your account. You are then taken to your department.

Note: the app only forces this screen when you sign in through /staff-login. Until the password is changed, the system refuses every staff action even if a page opens, so always sign in through Staff portal.

Signing out: press Sign out at the right of the staff bar. Always sign out on shared computers.

If you see "Welcome to the staff portal / available only to authorised staff": you are either not signed in or your role does not include that page. Press the button on the screen (Staff sign in or Open my department).

Customer accounts cannot sign in here. Staff accounts are created by the administrator — ask them if you do not have one.

Staff centre for an administrator: staff bar on top, one card per department.

A department dashboard (Reports & administration): function cards under On this page.

Press a card to open only that function. Use the Back to dashboard button to return and pick another. Anything you have started typing is kept when you switch functions.

2.4 Warehouse

Warehouse dashboard (warehouse manager).

Brand warehouses: one storage area per vehicle brand.

Open: staff bar → Warehouse (/warehouse). Roles: warehouse manager, clerk, picker, packer, dispatch, auditor; administrator and general manager (view only).

The warehouse has six brand storage areas — one for each vehicle brand. Parts are automatically assigned to the correct brand zone. Press a brand tile to focus on that brand; press ← Back to all warehouses to return.

Functions (cards):

Customer order fulfilment queue — Pick, pack & dispatch

Fulfilment queue: Start picking → Scan & pick → Start packing → Ready for dispatch → Mark dispatched. Handles every website order from picking through dispatch.

  1. Start picking (picker / warehouse manager).
  2. Scan & pick each item. The barcode scan is checked and stock is issued using FIFO (oldest stock first).
  3. Start packing (packer / warehouse manager).
  4. Ready for dispatch (packer, dispatch or warehouse manager).
  5. Mark dispatched (dispatch or warehouse manager).

Receive stock — Receive parts

Receive stock form. (Warehouse manager or clerk.)

  1. Choose Existing part (pick it from the list) or + Create new part (enter vehicle brand, part number / SKU, name, category, OEM / Aftermarket / Genuine, selling price, compatible models, years, engine, optional image URL). A new part is published online immediately.
  2. Choose the Dedicated storage area, Quantity, Batch / GRN number, Bin code, Supplier, Supplier reference and Unit cost (KSh).
  3. Save. Each delivery becomes its own stock batch.

Products created in Reports & administration start with zero stock. Stock is added only by receiving it here, so that every unit has a batch history.

Upload parts from CSV — Bulk receiving

Bulk CSV import. (Warehouse manager or clerk.) Press Download CSV template, fill it in, then Select completed CSV file and upload. Every row goes to its brand warehouse and its own FIFO batch.

Create preorder

Create preorder. Records demand for stock that has not arrived yet: customer name, phone, expected date, notes. Press Save preorder. Open preorders are listed under Open preorders.

Return / factory defect — Receive returns

Return / factory defect form. Choose the reason (Factory defect, Customer return, Wrong part) and record it. Defective parts go to quarantine. Open items appear under Open returns & defects.

Stock batches & labels

FIFO stock batches with Print label links. Shows FIFO stock batches. Barcode labels can be generated for received parts.

Rules to remember

  • You cannot edit a product's stock number directly. Receive new stock, or raise a Stock adjustment request (2.8) after a physical count.
  • If the system warns that stock needs reconciliation, stop selling that part and tell a manager — nothing is auto-corrected.

2.5 Sales counter (POS)

Sales counter dashboard (cashier).

Open: staff bar → Sales counter (/pos). Roles: cashier and administrator can sell; general manager can view.

Making a sale

Find a part (left) and Counter sale (right).

A cash sale ready to record: part added, customer name and cash received entered.

The receipt after pressing Record paid sale; press Print receipt.

  1. Find a part — type the part name, number or scan the barcode, press Search. The list shows availability (physical stock minus quantities reserved by online orders). Press Add to put a part on the sale.
  2. Counter sale panel — set the quantity (Qty) for each line, or press × to remove one.
  3. Enter Customer name (optional).
  4. Choose Payment method:
  5. Cash — enter Cash received. The screen shows the change.
  6. M-Pesa or Card — enter the Confirmed payment reference. This records a payment you have already received and checked yourself; the system does not charge or verify M-Pesa/card payments.
  7. Press Record paid sale.
  8. The receipt opens. Press Print receipt. Receipts are sales receipts, not tax invoices.

Recent receipts

Recent receipts: click one to reopen and reprint. Use Recent receipts to reopen and reprint a receipt (for example if the window was closed). Cashiers see their own receipts.

Important safety rules

General manager's view of the counter: inputs are greyed out (read-only).

  • If a "pending sale" warning appears, the result is uncertain. Retry the same sale with the same details to retrieve its receipt — never collect payment twice.
  • Do not start the same sale in two browser tabs.
  • If stock looks wrong, stop and tell the warehouse manager.
  • Completed sales cannot be edited. For a refund or correction, raise a request in Requests & approvals (2.8).
  • The page header shows approved refund credits for the day.

2.6 Garage jobs

Garage dashboard.

Open: staff bar → Garage jobs (/staff-garage). Roles: garage staff and administrator; general manager can view.

Use it to enter workshop bookings received by WhatsApp or at the counter, and to track repairs. It does not import WhatsApp messages.

New booking

New garage booking form. Open New booking and fill in customer name, phone, vehicle / model, registration or VIN, requested service and preferred date. Save to create a job card (with a job number).

Job cards

Job cards with notes and Move to … buttons. Each job card shows the job number, registration, customer, vehicle, service, status and a history of notes.

  1. Type in Progress / parts-required note (work done, customer authorisation reference, or parts needed).
  2. Press Add note to save the note only, or press Move to … to advance the status and save the note.
  3. A note is required to save or move a job. Notes can only be added, never edited.

Job progress order: Booked → Inspection → Awaiting customer / In progress → Ready → Completed. (From Inspection you can go to Awaiting customer or In progress; from Awaiting customer to In progress.)

Parts you write in notes do not reduce stock or charge the customer. Sell or issue parts through the warehouse or sales counter.

To cancel, reopen or correct a job, press Request a correction, cancellation or reopening (goes to Requests & approvals).

For drivers — "My Assigned Deliveries"

2.8 Requests & approvals

Requests & approvals dashboard.

Open: staff bar → Requests & approvals (/approvals). Available to every operational role.

Ordinary sales and receiving never need approval. Exceptions do. The approval chain is:

Staff member requests → General manager reviews → Administrator applies. A request made by the general manager goes straight to the administrator. A request made by the administrator still needs general manager review before the administrator applies it.

Nothing changes until the administrator applies it. Pending requests never change prices, stock or completed sales.

What each role can ask for

Role Request types
Cashier Price change, Refund, Sale correction (own counter sales only)
Garage staff Garage correction (cancel, reopen or correct a job)
Warehouse roles Stock adjustment
General manager, Administrator All types, including Order correction

Submitting a request

Known issue: the Submit an exception request form has no card on the dashboard in the current version, so it can't be opened yet — see 3.4. The steps below describe how it is meant to work.

  1. Open Submit an exception request.
  2. Choose the Request type. Depending on the type, also choose the Sale type (POS or ONLINE), the record, the field to change, the new value (for example Order status or Payment record status).
  3. Write the Reason and supporting reference — explain the original error and the exact correction.
  4. Press Submit for approval.

Following a request

Requests & decisions. Requests & decisions (card Requests & decisions) lists every request, who requested and reviewed it, and the notes. Reviewers add a Decision note and approve, reject or apply.

Refunds

  • An approved refund creates a credit only. It does not send money and does not return stock.
  • Pay the customer outside the system (M-Pesa, bank or cash). Then the administrator opens Approved refunds and uses Record completed external payout, entering the actual transfer reference / cash voucher. Never pay twice.
  • A stock return after a refund is a separate Stock adjustment request after a physical count.

Sales & customer accounts page (note: normal website header, no staff bar).

Card What it does

These documents are internal commercial records — not eTIMS tax invoices, and they do not reserve or dispatch stock.

2.10 ERP & payroll

ERP & payroll dashboard.

Prepare a salary record.

Salary records.

Open: staff bar → ERP & payroll (/payroll). Administrator and general manager only. Nobody else can see salary data.

Workflow: Administrator prepares → General manager reviews → Administrator approves.

  1. First use: the administrator presses Initialise payroll (creates empty tables only).
  2. Prepare a salary record (administrator): choose the Employee, Month, Basic pay, Regular cash allowances, Preferred payment method (Bank transfer or M-Pesa) and Tax residency. A Calculation preview shows NSSF, SHIF, housing levy, PAYE and net pay. Press Submit for general manager review.
  3. The general manager opens the record under Salary records, adds a Review note and approves or rejects it. The preparer cannot review their own record.
  4. The administrator gives final approval.
  5. After approval, press Print approved payslip (A5). In the print window choose A5, portrait, 100% scale, and turn off browser headers and footers.

Important: approval does not pay employees or remit statutory deductions. Payslips say "approved — payment not sent". Rejected records can be replaced; submitted amounts can't be edited. Rules cover February–December 2026 regular monthly cash pay only. A qualified person must verify the figures before real payments or tax filing.

2.11 My HR

My HR dashboard.

Apply for leave.

HR administration view (HR role).

Open: staff bar → My HR (/my-hr). Every staff member has it. For the HR role it is the only page and is titled HR administration.

Sections (jump links at the top): Leave applications · My payslips · Email · Employee documents · Medical insurance · Staff directory (managers/HR only).

  • Apply for leave: choose start and end dates, leave type (Annual, Sick, Unpaid), give a reason, press Submit leave application. Track it under My leave requests.
  • Leave approval flow: the request goes to HR, who adds a note and presses Forward to general manager; the general manager then presses Approve leave or Reject leave. A review note is required each time. No administrator approval is needed.
  • My approved payslips: press View payslip [period] to see it; Print / Save payslip as PDF (A5) to print. Only your own payslips appear.
  • Employee documents: staff can view their own. HR and the administrator upload private documents (choose staff member, document type, file up to 2 MB). Downloads are logged.
  • Medical insurance applications: HR creates a draft for a staff member, attaches supporting documents, and forwards it to the general manager. Internal approval does not enrol anyone with an insurer.
  • Email: placeholder only — no mailbox is connected.
  • First use: HR or the administrator may need to press Enable HR records once.

Reports & administration dashboard.

Add product.

Recent order pipeline.

Products table.

3.2 Troubleshooting

Problem What to do
"Welcome to the staff portal… available only to authorised staff" Sign in at /staff-login, or use Open my department if your role does not include that page.
"Change your temporary password" screen Press Change password; use at least 12 characters.
Cannot sign in Check email and password. Staff and customer accounts are separate. Ask the administrator to reset your password in Operations & staff → Employee access.
A page shows greyed-out / "viewing only" You are a general manager. Use Requests & approvals to propose changes.
Warehouse shows only one brand Your account is assigned to that brand (email begins with the brand name).
Receipt window closed before printing Sales counter → Recent receipts → reopen and Print receipt.
"Pending sale" warning at the counter Retry the same sale. Never take payment again.
"Stock reconciliation required" Stop selling that part. Tell the warehouse manager; stock needs a physical count and, if needed, a Stock adjustment request.
Proof of delivery blocked Customer's M-Pesa payment is still pending. Press Prompt Customer to Pay, then Refresh payment status.
"Enable HR records" / "Initialise payroll" / "Enable sales and finance records" buttons A one-time setup by the administrator. Ask them.
VIN rejected It must be 17 characters; no I, O or Q. If it still fails, search manually in the Shop.
Website is slow to load after being idle Reload the page and sign in again if asked.
Database error / /api/health shows "unavailable" Tell IT: the database connection or setup (migrations) needs attention.

3.3 Things that are not connected yet

So nobody is misled, these are not automatic at the moment:

  • Contact page form — "Send enquiry" does not send; use phone, email or WhatsApp.
  • Card and PayPal payments — selectable at checkout, but live charging needs provider credentials. M-Pesa online prompts also need provider credentials.
  • M-Pesa / card at the sales counter — you record a payment you have already verified; the system does not check it.
  • Refunds and payroll — approval records the decision only. No money is sent to customers or employees; statutory deductions are not remitted.
  • KRA eTIMS tax invoices — documents are internal records only.
  • Email (staff) — placeholder; no mailbox connected.
  • Branch counters — only the Main business location is active.
  • WhatsApp bookings — the website opens WhatsApp for the customer; bookings are not imported. Garage staff enter them manually.
  • Malware scanning of HR document uploads is not connected — upload only necessary files.

3.4 Known issues (for the administrator / IT)

These were found while preparing this manual by running the site against a test database. They are application problems, not user mistakes.

# Where What happens Effect
1 Requests & approvals The Submit an exception request form has no dashboard card (the card list expects a heading called "New request"). Staff cannot raise price, refund, stock, sale, order or garage correction requests from the screen.
2 Sales & customer accounts (/receivables) All customer / quotation / invoice panels are hidden whenever the Finance workspace is shown. Quotations, invoices and credit limits can't be used. Finance functions work.
3 Temporary password The staff session check does not report the "must change password" flag to the page, so the Change your temporary password screen doesn't appear when a page is opened directly. Server still blocks actions, but staff may see confusing errors. Signing in through Staff portal works correctly.
4 Public pages at about 1366 px wide The page is slightly wider than the screen (about 1450 px). The account and cart icons at the far right of the header can be pushed off-screen. Zoom out (Ctrl and minus) or widen the window.
5 Sales counter and Garage jobs Some headings and notes are dark text on a dark background. Hard to read; the buttons and inputs still work.
6 Brand warehouses Brand logo images show as broken pictures. Cosmetic.
7 New database setup pnpm db:migrate fails on a brand-new empty database at the last step (must_change_password column used before it is created). Only affects setting up a fresh database, not the live one.
8 Contact page "Send enquiry" button does nothing. Use phone, email or WhatsApp.
